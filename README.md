Header — "ZEPTO Analysis Dashboard": Title banner with Zepto branding, sets the context for the report.

KPI Cards (Top Row):
Total Revenue: 12bn — Sum of total revenue generated across all products (based on discounted selling price × quantity sold).
Total Product Sold: 796K — Aggregate count/units of products sold.
Average Discount Price: 7.62 — Average discount percentage applied across all products.
Average Selling Price: 14.19K — Mean of the discounted selling price across the catalog.
Number of Out of Stock Items: 453 — Count of SKUs currently marked outOfStock = True.

Filter Panel (Top Right):
Category slicer — Filters the entire dashboard by product category (All / Munchies / Chocolates & Candies / etc.).
Product Name slicer — Filters the dashboard down to a specific product.

"Category By Quality" (Bar Chart, left-middle): Shows product count distributed by category — e.g., Cooking Essentials, Munchies, and Chocolates & Candies lead with ~103K–106K, while Health & Hygiene has the fewest.

"Number of Out of Stock Item by Category" (Donut Chart, center-middle): Breaks down the 453 out-of-stock items by category — Cooking Essentials (64, 14.13%) and Munchies (64, 14%) have the highest stock-outs, followed by Chocolates & Candies, Biscuits, and Beverages (each ~45–46, ~9.9%)

"MRP by Discount Selling Price" (Line Chart, right-middle): Plots the relationship/trend between MRP and the discounted selling price, highlighting price volatility and discount depth across products (peaks up to 1.41M).

"Category By Quality" (Bar Chart, bottom-left): Shows revenue contribution by category — Chocolates & Candies and Ice Cream & Desserts lead with ~1.7bn each, while Fruits & Vegetables and Health & Hygiene contribute the least

"Top 10 Product by Quality" (Horizontal Bar Chart, bottom-right): Ranks the top 10 best-selling/highest-quantity products — led by Kellogg's Original Corn Flakes (4.1K), followed by MTR Roasted Vermicelli (3.8K), Pedigree Puppy Dry Dog Food (3.6K), and others.

Tools Used:
Data Source: Excel (zepto_v1.xlsx)
Visualization: Power BI (based on dashboard styling)

Key Insights:
Cooking Essentials, Munchies, and Chocolates & Candies are the top-stocked and top-selling categories.

Project Overview:
This project analyzes product-level data from Zepto, a quick-commerce grocery delivery platform, to uncover insights around revenue, pricing, discounts, stock availability, and category-wise performance. The analysis is visualized through an interactive Power BI/Tableau-style dashboard.
Out-of-stock issues are concentrated in the same high-volume categories, suggesting demand-supply gaps.
Chocolates & Candies and Ice Cream & Desserts generate the highest revenue despite not always having the highest product count
